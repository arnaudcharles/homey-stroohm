Charging a company car at home with STROOHM? This app shows, right in Homey, how much you charged and how much your employer refunds: this month, this year and since day one. No more exports rebuilt in Excel.

WHAT YOU GET
- kWh charged at home: this month, last month, this year and in total
- Exact refund due this month, as billed to your employer, and a projection for the whole month
- Refund pending (the current month, paid by Monta on the 1st) and total refunded
- Current refund rate
- Your real gain or loss: refund minus your electricity cost, per month, per year and in total
- Public and office charging paid by your company card: kWh and amount, and the share charged at home
- Every value can be shown as the device indicator and has its full history in Insights

ELECTRICITY COST
Use the tariff set in the STROOHM app, a fixed price, or a day/night (dual) tariff. With day/night, the app prices every kWh at the hour it was really charged (hourly data from Monta), or applies the average you choose: always at night, 50/50, always by day, or your own share.

BEFORE MONTA
If you were refunded through another platform before Monta, enter what you received per month or per year, and optionally the kWh charged at home and your public charging. Totals, gain/loss and home share then cover your whole history.

FLOWS
When: a home charging session ended, a public charging session ended, a month was closed, the refund rate changed, a monthly report is ready.
And: pending refund above an amount, this month's result is negative.
Then: refresh now, generate a monthly report (summary text + CSV).

MONTHLY REPORT
The app settings show a month-by-month table and export it as CSV for Excel or Google Sheets.

REQUIREMENTS
- A STROOHM driver account with a home charger (STROOHM runs on the Monta platform).
- API credentials (Client ID + Client secret), created on platform.stroohm.com. The pairing screen explains the steps.

PRIVACY
Your data stays on your Homey and is only exchanged with the Monta API. Nothing is sent to the developer.

The app does not control your charger and adds nothing to Homey Energy, so your consumption is never counted twice.

This is a community app. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by or supported by STROOHM or Monta.
